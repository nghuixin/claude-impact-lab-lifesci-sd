# Claude Community Impact Lab: Biotech & Life Sciences (San Diego)

Part II of the Claude Conversation series. Last month we talked about the future of AI in biotech and life sciences. Tonight we build.


Protect-Data Kit

A tool that helps life sciences teams avoid putting proprietary data into AI tools.

Team
Team name: JaJaRe
Members: Jared Chapman, Jay Singh, Renee Ng

Track and problem statement
Track: Protecting proprietary scientific data
User: Scientists, data managers and program staff in life sciences organizations who are starting to use AI assistants in day-to-day work.
Workflow: Before pasting text into an AI tool, the user runs it through the paste-checker, which flags sequences, structures, assay values, internal IDs, participant terms, PHI-shaped fields and secrets, and produces a redacted copy. When they are unsure whether something belongs in a tool at all, they answer four questions in the decision tree and get one of four outcomes. During the event, they practice on synthetic files instead of their own data.
Outcome: Fewer accidental disclosures of unpublished results, structures, sequences and participant data, and a shared vocabulary for deciding what can go where.
